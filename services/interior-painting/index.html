Our Painting Process

We follow a detailed process to ensure professional results every time:

  1. Free Consultation: We visit your home, discuss your vision, review color options, and provide a detailed written estimate.
  2. Surface Preparation: We clean walls, repair damage, sand rough spots, and apply primer to ensure paint adheres properly.
  3. Protection: We cover floors, furniture, and fixtures with drop cloths and plastic sheeting.
  4. Painting: Our painters apply two coats of premium paint using brushes, rollers, and sprayers for even coverage.
  5. Trim & Detail Work: We carefully paint trim, baseboards, doors, and windows with clean, sharp lines.
  6. Cleanup: We remove all protective coverings, clean up thoroughly, and conduct a final inspection with you.
  7. Follow-Up: We're available for any touch-ups or questions after project completion.

Pricing Factors

Interior painting costs vary based on several factors. Understanding these helps you budget accurately:

  • Room size: Larger rooms require more paint and labor time.
  • Ceiling height: Tall or vaulted ceilings increase project complexity.
  • Surface condition: Damaged walls need repair before painting, adding to the cost.
  • Number of colors: Multiple colors require more precise cutting and taping.
  • Paint quality: Premium paints cost more but offer better coverage and durability.
  • Trim and details: Painting trim, doors, and cabinets adds time and precision work.

Most interior painting projects in Cumming range from $2,000 to $6,000 depending on scope. We provide transparent, itemized estimates with no hidden fees. For detailed pricing guidance, read our interior painting cost guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does interior painting take?

Most single-room projects take 1-2 days. Whole-home painting typically requires 3-7 days depending on size and scope. We provide a detailed timeline in your estimate and communicate any schedule changes promptly.

Do I need to move furniture?

We can work around most furniture. For best results, we recommend moving smaller items and clearing wall space. We'll cover and protect remaining furniture with drop cloths and plastic sheeting.

What paint brands do you use?

We work with premium brands including Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore. These paints offer excellent coverage, durability, and color retention. We'll recommend the best product for your specific needs and budget.

Can you help me choose paint colors?

Yes! We offer free color consultations. Our team will review your existing decor, lighting, and preferences to suggest colors that complement your space. We can also provide sample swatches to test on your walls. Check out our 2024 paint color trends guide for inspiration.

Do you repair damaged walls before painting?

Absolutely. We handle drywall repair, crack filling, hole patching, and texture matching as part of our surface preparation. Proper wall prep is essential for a professional finish. Learn more in our wall preparation guide.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Resilience Solutions is fully licensed and insured for your protection. We carry both general liability and workers' compensation insurance. You'll have peace of mind knowing you're working with a legitimate, professional painting contractor.
