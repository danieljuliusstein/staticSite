Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I repaint my home's interior?

Most interior paint lasts 5-10 years depending on room traffic, paint quality, and wall preparation. High-traffic areas like kitchens and hallways may need refreshing sooner, while bedrooms can often go longer. If you notice fading, scuff marks, or peeling, it's time for a fresh coat.

What's the best paint finish for bathrooms?

Semi-gloss or satin finishes work best in bathrooms because they resist moisture and are easier to clean. These sheens help prevent mildew buildup and stand up to humidity better than flat or matte finishes.

How much does a typical interior painting project cost?

In the Cumming area, expect to pay $2-$6 per square foot for professional interior painting, depending on ceiling height, wall condition, and paint quality. A standard 12x12 room typically costs $400-$900 including labor and materials. Check out our detailed cost guide for more specifics.

Do I need to prime before painting?

Yes, in most cases. Primer seals porous surfaces, covers stains, and ensures even color coverage. If you're painting over a similar color with quality paint-and-primer products, you may skip it, but new drywall, patched areas, or dramatic color changes always require primer.

Can I paint over wallpaper?

While technically possible, it's not recommended. Wallpaper can bubble, peel, or create texture issues under paint. For best results, remove wallpaper completely, repair any wall damage, prime the surface, then paint. This ensures a smooth, long-lasting finish.

How do I choose between hiring a pro and DIY painting?

Consider the project scope, your skill level, and time availability. Small rooms with good wall condition are DIY-friendly. Larger projects, high ceilings, extensive prep work, or areas requiring special finishes benefit from professional expertise. Pros also carry insurance and warranty their work, giving you peace of mind.
